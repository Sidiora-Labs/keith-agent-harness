# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Keith community a welcoming,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, respectful,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive community includes:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from
  mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Helping new contributors participate without gatekeeping.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project spaces, reviews, or community support.
- Conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and may take fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull
requests, documentation, or other contributions that conflict with this Code of
Conduct. Moderation decisions should be explained when doing so is safe and
appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Keith community spaces and when someone is
officially representing the project in public or private spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the
repository owners through the contact methods on the
[Machine City organization profile](https://github.com/machinecity). Do not post
conduct reports publicly or use a security advisory for ordinary community
disputes. If no private project contact is available, use GitHub's platform
reporting tools.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. Maintainers
will respect the privacy and safety of the reporter and may issue a warning,
require corrective action, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently
remove someone from project spaces depending on the circumstances.
